About

I build practical AI systems where mathematics, algorithms, and real engineering meet. My recent work spans smart-wearable audio, multi-channel speech enhancement, LLM evaluation & benchmarking, and AI systems.

I care about reproducible experiments, clear documentation, and explainable technical choices — starting from small prototypes to validate ideas, then maturing the effective parts into long-term projects.
